# Outreach SOP

## First touch

Use the approved standard outreach copy. Do not rewrite the positioning, claims, or offer unless the operator explicitly requests a change. Personalize only with verified company facts.

## Batch sending

Before any collective send, show the recipient list, email addresses, template, exclusions, duplicate check, and channel. Wait for explicit confirmation.

## Follow-up

- First outreach unanswered for at least seven days: propose a second touch if a usable channel exists.
- Second touch unanswered for another seven days: suggest a channel change or pause.
- Positive reply, meeting request, information request, or referral: summarize and propose the next action.
- Out-of-office: record the return timing and wait.
- Bounce: mark contact invalid and find another channel.

## Meetings

External program meetings are handled by the designated Program Manager. The agent prepares company research, questions, and briefing material, but must not promise attendance by an unauthorized person or accept a meeting on behalf of the team without confirmation.

## VC partnerships

Before sharing extensive resources, ask for:

- Portfolio overview, especially AI-native companies
- Investment stages and geography
- Priority sectors
- Expected collaboration model
- Referral, credits, technical support, or ecosystem goals

## Email response style

Professional, concise, factual, and open-ended. Do not promise credits, pricing, data-retention guarantees, or technical capabilities that have not been approved.
